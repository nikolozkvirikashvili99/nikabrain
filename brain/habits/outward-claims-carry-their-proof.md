**Rule:** before handing over anything another person will read, list its
factual claims and give each a source: a command you ran, a `file:line`, a
fetched page, or the word "guess". Unsourced claims get measured or cut.

**Why:** invented counts ("~30 consumers", when a grep found 27), precedents
taken from a ticket *title*, and claims created by *shortening* a correct
draft each cost a round and some trust. Measuring was cheap every time.

**How to apply:**
- Draft → claims table (claim → ran / read / cited / inferred).
- Inferred claims get a quick check or a "probably".
- Counts come from a command, never from memory.
- When a later check overturns something Nika already has, say so first.
- Match the team's own length: read 2–3 of their PRs/tickets first ([[pr-body-sized-to-the-diff]]).

**Signal:** the text goes to a PR, ticket, channel, client or manager; or Nika asks "are you sure?".
**Next time:** run the claims pass before the first draft is shown.
