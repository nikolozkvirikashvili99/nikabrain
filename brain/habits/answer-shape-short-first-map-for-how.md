**Rule:**
1. "What do I do" → at most 5 steps or 10 lines, then one line offering more.
2. "How does X work" / "are we using X" → one table (thing, what it does,
   status), then an honest list of what is unused, unproven or missing.
3. For an unfamiliar mechanism, **one sustained analogy beats jargon**, then
   go straight to the real thing ("in React this is the reconciler…").
4. A "done" report opens with one plain sentence (what was wrong, what
   changed), then the proof in one line. No jargon tables.

**Why:** walls of prose get sent back. A map without gaps hides the
difference between "instructed" and "actually enforced or proven".

**How to apply:** use honest status words. "Tested by piping JSON, not fired
live yet" beats "the hook protects you".
